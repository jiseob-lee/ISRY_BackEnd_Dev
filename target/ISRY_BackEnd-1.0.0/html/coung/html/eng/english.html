Uss Guidelines

What does Youth Cyber Counseling Center do?

Youth Cyber Counseling Center, managed by the Ministry of Gender Equality and Family and commissioned by the Korea Youth Counseling and Welfare Institute, is a professional counseling center for teens and parents.
As online communcation becomes familiar to teens, we provide online counseling service to young adults who experience family conflict, friendship issue, school interruption, runaway, Internet addiction, career and academic problems to help them grow healthy.

Who can get counseling services?

Our service is available to teens aged 9 to 24 and their parents.

What is the operating hours?

We are open 24/7, 365 days a year.

What service do you provide?

We offer various services such as online live chat counseling, message board counseling, online psychological exams, and problem solving guidelines.

Live chat counseling
A counselee can have a one-on-one talk with a professional counselor.

Message board counseling
A counselee can ask for a counseling advice from a professional counselor anonymously by leaving a message on the message board on our website.

Comment Counseling
It is an open counseling page where a counselee explains his or her situation within 150 letters and several counselors give advice to it.

Solrobot Counseling
A counselee finds a similar problems he or she faces in a character from a game or animated movie, and get counseling services from a virtual counselor character.

Online Psychological Exams
We provide teens and parents with various psychological tests such as interpersonal relationships, career/academic, personality/emotion, etc.

E-um -e
We offer parenting classes for parents who are unable to participate in offline class so they can improve relationships with their children and develop themselves as well.

Definition and advantage of online counseling

Professional counseling services/activities in cyberspace; counselees can get counseling services using their smartphone and laptop without the limitation of place and time.
